About NAVAN Marine

Technical thinking. Practical design support.

NAVAN Marine is an independent marine design and engineering consultancy concept focused on supporting ship and yacht projects with clear, coordinated and build-conscious technical deliverables.

We aim to help clients move from design intent to coordinated engineering documentation through a structured approach, attention to detail and collaboration with multidisciplinary teams.

Whether the requirement is piping design, 3D model development, preliminary hull-form work or retrofit support, each scope is defined around the vessel, project stage and client requirements.

Our focus

  • Ship and yacht piping design support
  • 3D modelling and production documentation
  • Ship and yacht structure design support
  • Naval architecture calculations and design studies
  • Marine retrofit engineering support
  • Coordination with client and multidisciplinary teams

Marine design services

Ship & Yacht Mechanical & Piping Design

02 / HVAC

03 / STRUCTURE

04 / 3D DESIGN

3D Modelling & Production Drawings

05 / NAVAL ARCHITECTURE

Naval Architecture Calculations

FEED & Feasibility Studies, Technical specification, General arrangement, Equipment number valulation, Tonnage calculation, Midship setion, profile and deck plan, Resistance and powering calculation, Accommodation layout and Galley layout.

Marine Retrofit Consultancy

Design support for vessel modification and retrofit projects, including survey-input review, layout updates and multidisciplinary coordination.

07 / PROJECT SUPPORT

Engineering Documentation

Technical drawing updates, report preparation, model-based quantities and documentation support aligned with client workflows.

How we work

A clear process from enquiry to delivery

Understand

STEP 02

Define
